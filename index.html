Size guide

Sizes that leave room to grow

School sizes are chest measurements in inches. We cut a little long in the sleeve and hem so the uniform lasts the year.

Enter the child's height and we will suggest a starting size. Confirm with a fitting at the shop or against a uniform that fits now.

The shop

Thirty years of first days at school

Arora Uniforms opened on Kalkaji Main Road in 1996, a short walk from Desh Bandhu College. Three decades later the shop still cuts its own patterns, keeps every school's shade card on file, and does its own alterations on the premises.

Moving the order book online does not change the work. It only means a parent in Alaknanda or an office in Nehru Place can place an order on a Sunday night and have it ready for pickup by Wednesday.
